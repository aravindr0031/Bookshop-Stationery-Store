The Beginning

From a dusty attic
to a literary sanctuary.

Our story began in 2010 when our founders discovered an abandoned collection of first editions in a Victorian attic. Realizing the magic these forgotten stories held, they decided to create a space where books could be celebrated, not just sold.

The Discovery

Our Philosophy

What We Stand For

At The Bookery, we operate on a set of core principles that guide everything from our book selection to how we serve our community.

Diverse Voices

We actively source books from marginalized authors, translated works, and independent presses to ensure our shelves reflect the beautiful diversity of our world.

Sustainability

Our packaging is 100% recyclable, and we partner with environmental initiatives to plant a tree for every 10 books sold in our store or online.

Community First

We host free reading programs for local schools, provide meeting spaces for book clubs, and champion local authors through dedicated shelf space.

Curation with Care

No algorithms here. Every book on our display tables is hand-picked by a staff member who has read it, loved it, and wants to share it.

Join our community newsletter

Get weekly updates on new arrivals, staff picks, and upcoming events.
